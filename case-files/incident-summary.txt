BRIGHTLINE SUPPLY CO. - INCIDENT SUMMARY            CONFIDENTIAL
Incident #: IR-2026-007
Prepared by: Dana Whitfield, IT Manager

WHAT HAPPENED
- Friday, March 20, 2026, 7:40 p.m.: An attacker logged in to the shared
  file server using an employee's password, which had been stolen in a
  phishing email.
- The attacker copied three files from the \\FILESERVER\Shared folder:
    1. customers.csv          (customer list exported from the online store)
    2. employees.csv          (HR export, saved to the shared folder by mistake)
    3. supplier-terms.txt     (confidential pricing agreement with a supplier)
- Saturday, March 21, 2026, 9:15 a.m.: IT discovered the activity in the
  server logs and confirmed the files were taken. This is the DISCOVERY DATE.
- The files were not encrypted.

WHAT WE KNOW ABOUT THE DATA
- Copies of all three files are in the "leaked" folder for your analysis.
- No person appears in both customers.csv and employees.csv.
- Brightline sells office supplies. It is not a hospital, doctor's office,
  health insurer or bank.
- Brightline is a private company (not traded on the stock market).

YOUR ROLE
You are Brightline's new security analyst. Leadership needs to know which
laws apply, who must be notified, and by when. Legal counsel will make the
final call, but they need your facts first.
